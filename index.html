About me

About me

I’m a Data Insight Analyst with experience across business intelligence, reporting and automation. I enjoy turning complex data into clear insights and building practical solutions that help teams understand performance, improve processes and make better decisions.

I work mainly with Power BI, SQL, Python and the Microsoft Power Platform, combining technical skills with a strong understanding of business needs. I particularly enjoy projects where I can take a manual or complicated process and make it simpler, faster and easier to use.

I also enjoy working closely with different teams to understand how data is actually used day to day. For me, a good solution is not just technically correct — it should also be clear, useful and easy for people to work with.

Outside of data, I’m naturally curious and always learning something new.
Fun fact: I speak both Italian and English, and I’m the kind of person who will probably try to automate a repetitive task before doing it twice.
